A small workshop for paperwork

Every document has somewhere to go — this is where it goes first.

Five stations, nineteen tools, for the ordinary business of files: converting formats, binding pages into a single stack, rearranging what's inside, signing and stamping, and making a scan readable again. Everything runs right here in your browser — nothing you feed in is ever sent anywhere.
