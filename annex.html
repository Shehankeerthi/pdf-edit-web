The Annex — Stations 05–14

Ten more tools for the odder jobs.

Splitting, compressing, signing, watermarking, numbering, cropping, repairing, scanning, and converting — all running on-device, same as the rest of the shop. Pick a tool from the list.

Station 05

Break a PDF into individual pages, or cut it into custom ranges. Everything is bundled into a zip for download.

Station 11

HTML to PDF

Paste HTML markup or plain text below. It's rendered exactly as written, then captured into a paginated PDF.

Station 12

Scan to PDF

Use your device camera (or upload photos) to capture pages, arrange them in order, and bind them into a single PDF.

Click a page to remove it. Pages are bound in the order shown.
